Athlete & Season Setup

For a mixed-age group, the program is built to the youngest age in the range. Safe for the youngest means safe for everyone older in the group, not the reverse.
Novice: still building fundamentals, conservative loading. Developing: solid fundamentals, moderate intensity. Advanced: full intensity/volume, complex lifts.
Readiness check: if an athlete can't do 5 clean bodyweight pull-ups with no kipping, check this box. Vertical-pull work then uses lat pulldowns, band-assisted pull-ups, and slow negatives until they're ready. It only affects vertical pulling, not the rest of the program.
Vertical-pull work defaults to band-based options that need no bar at all. Check this to add real pull-ups/chin-ups/negatives into the rotation too, for extra variety (full gym is unaffected either way).
Leave unchecked if your gym has no medicine or slam balls. Throws, slams, and ball-based core work are then swapped for equipment-free or cable/landmine options.
Strength: periodized, progressive loading, 2–3 days/week. Dryland: lighter, fixed-effort, higher frequency. Mobility/Core: short, focused 10–15 min sessions built around just that one area.
Five routines (A: Core, B: Upper Body, C: Leg Power, D: Hips, Glutes & Kick, E: Rotation, Streamline & Mobility), differentiated by skill tier. At 5 days/week every day is different, and the order shifts one day each week so swimmers who don't come every day still get all five. Overrides the usual periodized Dryland rotation.
Every session runs on one clock: a 2-minute warm-up, then a new exercise every 60 seconds (45 at the 10-minute setting, 50 at 15). The plan leaves 2-3 minutes for lining up and transitions, so it finishes inside the window. The work part of each minute grows every 4 weeks (about 25, 30, 35, then 40 seconds) instead of adding time, and each exercise has two options per level that switch every 4 weeks.
Starts at the tier selected above and steps up one AG level every N weeks, capping at AG4. It never regresses and never advances past AG4. If the season isn't long enough to reach AG4, it just advances as far as it gets. Days affected by an advance are marked in that week's day titles.
